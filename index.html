How I Work

My approach comes from the floor, not the office.

Start on the floor.

Before I build anything, I collect the paperwork, shadow operators, and do the checks alongside them. The tribal knowledge that never makes it onto a form is where good system design starts.

If it doesn't work for the operator, it doesn't work.

If a check takes longer to document than to perform, or makes the line more stressful, I rethink the approach.

Earn trust by showing up.

During rollout, I work as part of the team: fixing issues quickly, jumping in when a line falls behind, and making sure operators know QA is on their side.

Build the handoffs, not just the forms.

Our Work Order Management form started with four fields and was shaped by supervisor feedback over months. It now launches tasks, tracks production, and triggers sanitation's CIP automatically, replacing radio calls where details got lost.
